## Spec

**P1 — Check the entire funding root before a doctor reverses it.** `billing.service.ts:363–372` uses scope-filtered `state.corrections` to detect refunds, but `funds.ts:63–65` invalidates every lot for the root. A wholly-own original receipt may be released/reallocated to another doctor, then released/refunded there. With no remaining foreign active allocation, the original doctor can reverse it: foreign refunds are hidden, and foreign available credit is not scope-checked. This violates “own performed work only” and “Refunds return available patient credit only.” Use unfiltered facts for invariants and authorize every affected allocation/credit before root invalidation. Also retain historical own-charge attribution when all allocations were released: otherwise first reversal succeeds with empty `chargeIds`, disappears from own-work history, and identical retry returns 403. Pending builder fix.

**P2 — Retain charge attribution independently of whole-event visibility.** `billing.service.ts:284–287` derives a source's charge IDs from visible correction events. A doctor-manager can cancel a mixed-performer plan; each ordinary doctor sees their attributed source but not the whole cancellation. Their refund request receives empty `chargeIds`, becomes hidden by the own-work filter, and cannot be approved or retried. The contract requires concerned-doctor approval for own performed work. Preserve exact source-charge attribution without exposing the mixed event. Pending builder fix.

**P2 — Keep an approved credit through routine plan completion.** `billing.service.ts:815–819` compares the credit's `acceptedRevision` with the latest plan revision carrying acceptance. Routine completion appends a new revision with the same acceptance and prices (`visit.service.ts:129–149`), so a pre-completion credit becomes unreconciled and invoice issue fails. Financial adjustments must not silently change clinical scope, and routine lifecycle progress must not invalidate an explicit financial reduction. Compare agreement identity/content rather than lifecycle revision. Pending builder fix.

**P2 — Accept the aggregate range for final charges.** `packages/contracts/src/index.ts:1056` reuses the per-component `exactAmountSchema` for `finalCharge`, limiting it to 15 whole digits. A valid agreed charge sums base plus up to 50 extras and exceeds that limit; #20's retained regression has KWD `originalMinor:50999999999999999949`. A lower final charge of `50000000000000000.000` is therefore rejected on credit/cancellation despite being representable in the exact ledger. Preserve the already approved aggregate range rather than narrowing valid financial corrections. Pending builder fix.
