# Standalone consultation integration — decision session

2026-10-26. Owner explicitly invoked repository `grill-with-docs` to resolve the consultation gap. Scope: read-only investigation, focused interview and domain/planning documentation; no application implementation, ticket publication, migration or production work. ## Settled behavior

Consultation is independent of a treatment plan; existing/new patient, optional appointment, default price with authorized individual override. Full-only payment may be recorded at booking or later, only when actually paid. Latest owner correction: **paid consultation enters the doctor's share immediately, even before examination; an unpaid consultation becomes patient debt only when the patient enters the doctor's room**. Consultation may end with an unfinished note. Use normal receipts/refund approval, not a special cancellation process. No dummy plan.

Round1 owner answers: **reserve prepayment for that consultation**, not free credit for other care; **continue immediate treatment in the same clinical encounter**, with an independent consultation charge and treatment linked to its accepted plan. No forced end/start for the same attendance. Owner additionally requires consultation to count toward **doctor share**; calculation basis/rate configuration are not yet settled. This additional requirement is not evidence of existing doctor-share implementation.

Round2 owner clarification: doctor share means compensation for a doctor working on percentage rather than fixed salary. Consultation income joins clinic income used for calculating the share. The manager sets a rate per doctor, shared by that doctor's consultations and treatments. Rate configuration/scope is settled. The phrase **total clinic income** is still ambiguous between the doctor's own generated income and a pool of every doctor's income; do not silently equate those. Whether income means collected money or unpaid due charges also remains unconfirmed. No salary/payroll payment module is authorized by this compensation distinction.

Round3 clarification resolves revenue scope: **clinic income generated through that doctor**, including consultation/treatment, not other doctors' pooled income. Collected/settled-versus-unpaid basis (Q6) and compensation visibility (Q7) remain unanswered; do not infer acceptance from the scope clarification.

Historical Round4: Q6 initially approved settlement for provided care, Q7 manager/concerned doctor only. The later correction below supersedes the performed-care prerequisite **for consultation share**. Prior-credit application must still be counted once; normal source-linked corrections/refunds apply. No payroll execution requested.

Historical Round5: freeze rate at service start, changes prospective. Treatment retains this rule. Immediate pre-entry consultation-share recognition creates the consultation-specific rate-timing question below; do not silently use a future room-entry rate for a share already recorded at payment.

Latest correction after the final playback: consultation payment enters the doctor's share as soon as actual payment is recorded; it need not wait for the patient to enter or for care to be provided. Unpaid consultation is debt only after entry to the doctor's room. Funds remain dedicated to the consultation and must not generate another share/payment on entry. This supersedes previous consultation wording excluding unperformed advances. Room entry needs an explicit recorded application event; do not infer physical entry from opening details.

2026-10-27, owner Q9 answer: **freeze the consultation rate at payment or entry to the doctor's room, whichever occurs first**. Later rate settings do not recalculate it. If payment comes first, recognize share once at that frozen rate and create no additional share/debt on entry; if entry comes first, record the unpaid debt/frozen rate and recognize share only when actually paid. The owner subsequently confirmed the consolidated final understanding with **“ok”**. The decision session is complete; this is planning/domain-contract approval, not implementation authorization.

The glossary now defines Consultation and Consultation charge and distinguishes care charges, payment, credit and allocation. These are domain definitions of intended behavior, not evidence of current API support.

## Approved integration

Owner confirmed the final playback on2026-10-27 after the corrected payment/debt distinction and Q9 rate rule. This section is the approved integration direction; historical rounds above explain superseded wording, not competing rules.

Next-stage preparation: [local implementation scope and UI ownership map](consultation-implementation-scope.md). It proposes backend/contracts → frontend logic → existing UI bindings, with acceptance scenarios; no tickets published or implementation authorized by preparing it.

1. **Standalone service, shared encounter.** Give consultation its own identity, agreed price/currency, responsible doctor, lifecycle and optional appointment. Starting it creates/uses the clinical encounter without a plan. If treatment follows immediately, explicitly add/start procedures from the accepted plan in the same open encounter. Consultation completion, procedure completion and ending the encounter are distinct; do not force a new visit or prematurely complete the appointment when care continues.
2. **One clinical-note lifecycle.** Reuse encounter notes, authorship/finalization/corrections and derived pending-note tasks. Ending care can leave a draft. Do not introduce a separate consultation note store or duplicate the note when treatment follows.
3. **One financial ledger, distinct charge sources.** Consultation debt begins only on recorded entry to the doctor's room. Publish the consultation's single charge/recorded entry atomically; prior full payment covers it without a new balance or second share. Extend charge-source/projection to consultation versus treatment; no phantom plan, copied money subsystem or client-derived balances. Preserve patient/currency/doctor and correction history across reports/exports/historical snapshots.
4. **Dedicated paid funds.** Pre-entry full payment produces the normal receipt and consultation-linked funds, not free credit for another charge. **Include it immediately in the responsible doctor's consultation share.** Apply it to the consultation's charge once on room entry; no second receipt/income/share. Unpaid bookings are not debt before entry. Full-only consultation payment and ordinary treatment installments remain. Adapt normal release/refund/approval/correction machinery, not a separate cancellation policy.
5. **Narrow financial authority.** Honor authorized doctor/reception payment entry under relevant capabilities/patient scope. Pre-entry doctor access is restricted to their designated consultation/funds, not generic deposits or another doctor's funds. Paid consultation/share carries its responsible-doctor attribution; ordinary treatment uses its actual performer. Preserve explicit patient assignment, non-doctor clinical boundaries, staff redaction and historical/account restrictions. Payment entry does not assert room entry or start clinical care.
6. **Doctor remuneration reporting.** Manager sets per-doctor rate common to consultations/treatment. Consultation rate freezes at **earlier payment/room entry**; treatment rate at start. Consultation money enters share immediately on recorded payment, including pre-entry; provided treatment follows its actual-settlement basis. Do not double count original credit and its later use, or consultation payment and later entry. Source-linked releases/refunds/reversals affect the relevant base once, not unrelated deposits or both release and later refund. Preserve rates/history; manager/concerned doctor only. Exact currency-separated calculation; no rounded payout/payroll feature or fabricated zero for missing rate configuration.
7. **Layer ownership and verification.** Backend/contracts/logic precede final UI binding; #60 is not hidden ownership of these new backend features. Verify standalone consultation, no appointment, immediate treatment in same encounter, immediate share on pre-entry payment, no pre-entry unpaid debt, debt/coverage on recorded room entry, rate changes, repeated/concurrent commands, no duplicate note/charge/receipt/share, reserved-fund isolation and scoped output/history/corrections. Use fictional isolated data under separate implementation authorization. No tickets/migrations/services from planning approval.

Implementation should retain the modular monolith and existing Query/RHF/React/Zustand boundaries. The ADR records why shared encounter/ledger extension was chosen over separate visits/parallel billing. Exact schemas/routes/storage are engineering details to specify against current issue criteria, not fresh product decisions or an excuse for a new hypothetical interview.

Concrete acceptance examples (fictional, planned not executed): consultation500 at40%, paid before entry → receipt500/share200 immediately; change rate to50%, later entry → same rate40%/share200, no second receipt/share. Unpaid consultation500 at room entry with40% → due500/share0; change rate to50%, later pay500 → due0/share200 at retained40%. Normal approved refund/reversal follows original linked money/share entries, not the new rate.
