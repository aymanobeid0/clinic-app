# A3 — Recognize settled treatment income at the doctor's frozen rate

Status: owner-approved and published 2026-10-27. A1 execution explicitly authorized; dependent stages require prerequisite human-approved merge.

## Parent

Spec #1, approved standalone consultation amendment (ADR0002).

## What to build

The manager and concerned doctor can inspect the percentage-based doctor's own treatment share alongside consultation share, preserving the distinct recognition timing of treatment and consultation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reuse A1's manager-controlled per-doctor remuneration mode/rate. Treatment freezes the rate at procedure start; later settings apply prospectively. Missing settings are explicit and pre-existing unsnapshotted work is not silently assigned today's rate or a fabricated zero.
- [ ] Treatment recognizes only actual settlement against provided care attributed to that performer. Unstarted plans, unpaid debt and freely available advances are not treatment share. Consultation retains A2's immediate-payment exception.
- [ ] Existing credit plus new money counts once when applied to provided treatment; receiving the original deposit must not also create treatment share. Repeat/multi-visit procedure starts never freeze twice or duplicate shares.
- [ ] Preserve source-linked approved adjustments, releases, refunds and reversals at the original rate, with exact currency-separated calculation and retained history. Release and later refund cannot reduce the same share twice.
- [ ] Report only income generated by that doctor, not other doctors' pooled income. Manager/concerned-doctor access only; staff/unrelated doctors, exports and retained cutoff snapshots cannot leak remuneration. Historical-only accounts gain no active reporting permissions.
- [ ] No salary calculation, payroll/payout workflow, rounding patient money or second financial ledger. Current financial reports and consultation shares remain coherent.
- [ ] Authenticated isolated fictional persistence tests cover start40%/later setting50%, installments, credit/new money, multiple visits, performer isolation, correction chains and cutoff history. Actual contracts/OpenAPI and affected shared financial regressions pass.
