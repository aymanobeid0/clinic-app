# Standalone consultations within shared encounters and billing

Status: accepted by owner, 2026-10-27, after final shared-understanding confirmation. Planning/integration approval only; implementation is separately authorized.

Model consultation independently of a treatment plan, extending the existing encounter/common ledger so accepted treatment can follow in the same visit. Consultation payment stays dedicated and enters the doctor's share immediately; unpaid consultation becomes debt only at recorded room entry, with no second payment/share then; freeze its rate at the earlier payment/room-entry event. This avoids dummy plans, forced new visits and duplicate note/money systems while requiring deliberate source/command/projection extensions; the [approved integration](../planning/consultation-integration.md#approved-integration) records the scope, evidence and verification requirements.
